# Writing Style

Fold a qualification into the sentence itself as plain prose, or drop it — no parenthetical asides. Show emphasis through word order or a flat declarative statement, not bold text.

# Git

- Follow Conventional Commits for commit messages
  - Prefix in English (feat, fix, docs, refactor, test, chore)
  - Write the subject and body in Japanese
- When a body is needed, separate it from the subject with a blank line and describe the details
